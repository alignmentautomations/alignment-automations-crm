Kickoff & Intake

Day 1

Send the client ONE intake form to collect all of this:

  • ClientBusiness basics: legal name, brand name, service area, trades/services, hours
  • ClientLogo, brand colors, 6–12 photos of real work

02

Accounts & Infrastructure

Day 1–2

All on accounts you manage.

  • YouDomain registered or access delegated; DNS configured
  • YouGoogle Business Profile claimed & verified — or created new if none exists
  • YouGBP corrected: categories, service area, hours, phone, website link, photos uploaded

  • YouSMS / telephony provider set up for missed-call text-back

  • YouBooking tool (Cal.com) connected to client's calendar
  • YouLead-logging destination set up + internal notification channel

Don't-miss compliance items

A2P 10DLC registration (Phase 02) — US carriers require registration for business texting. Skip it and missed-call text-backs can be silently blocked. Start Day 1.

Email authentication (SPF / DKIM / DMARC) — without it, automated follow-ups land in spam, which kills the value prop. Non-negotiable per client.
